    ∫01∫x1ey2 dy dx=∫01∫0yey2 dx dy=∫01yey2 dy=(e−1)/2. \int_0^1\int_x^1 e^{y^2}\,dy\,dx =\int_0^1\int_0^y e^{y^2}\,dx\,dy =\int_0^1 ye^{y^2}\,dy=(e-1)/2. ∫01​∫x1​ey2dydx=∫01​∫0y​ey2dxdy=∫01​yey2dy=(e−1)/2.
